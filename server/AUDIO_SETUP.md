# AUDIO SETUP

## Audio Directory Structure
The audio files are organized into the following directories based on the country of origin:

- **Spain**: Contains audio files for the listening exercises from Spain.
- **Mexico**: Contains audio files for the listening exercises from Mexico.
- **Argentina**: Contains audio files for the listening exercises from Argentina.
- **Colombia**: Contains audio files for the listening exercises from Colombia.

## Adding Audio Files
To add audio files for the 5 IBDP Paper 2 listening exercises, follow these steps:

1. Identify the appropriate directory based on the country.
2. Use clear and descriptive filenames that include the exercise number and country name. For example: `exercise1_spain.mp3`.
3. Place the audio files in the corresponding country directory.

## Recording Guidelines
When recording audio files, please adhere to the following guidelines:

- Use a quality microphone to ensure clear audio.
- Record in a quiet environment to minimize background noise.
- Maintain consistent volume levels throughout the recording.
- Speak clearly and at a steady pace.

## Speaker Requirements
For the speaker(s) involved in the recordings, please ensure they meet these criteria:

- Native speakers of the language from the corresponding country (Spain, Mexico, Argentina, or Colombia).
- Experience in voice recording or narration is preferred.
- Ability to pronounce words clearly and accurately, reflecting regional accents where appropriate.

By following these instructions, we can ensure high-quality audio files for our IBDP listening exercises.
